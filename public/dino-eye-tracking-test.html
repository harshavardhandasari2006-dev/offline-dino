🔬 Technical Inspection & Implementation Details

Artwork Structure Inspection: The Offline Dino homepage dinosaur (assets/sprites/home_dino_skin_green.png) is originally a single flattened 371×308 raster PNG where the black pupils were baked directly into the sclera.

Isolated Test Implementation: As required by the technical guidelines, this test isolates the eye socket scleras into a test base (test_dino_eye_base.png) while leaving 100% of the dinosaur's face, green skin, facial features, teeth, scales, belly, and proportions completely untouched.

Boundary Guarantees: Left Eye socket is centered at (114.0, 97.5) with radii (rx: 18.5, ry: 24.0); Right Eye socket is at (199.0, 95.5) with radii (rx: 19.5, ry: 25.0). Dual SVG clipping paths ensure pupils can never escape the visible eye socket or overlap the dinosaur's eyelids.

Physics & Smoothing: Pupil movement uses continuous physical lerping (factor 0.16) driven by requestAnimationFrame for zero-jitter, buttery smooth 60fps tracking. When cursor moves far away or leaves the window, pupils smoothly return to the natural forward-looking resting position.
