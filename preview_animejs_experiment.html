📘 4. Natural Locomotion Technical Report & Walking Feasibility Analysis
1. Identified Causes of Unnatural Floating & Sliding
  • Bounding Box Left-Padding Jitter: When extracted, each frame was positioned at `minX = 7`. In stride extension poses (frames 2, 4, 13), the dinosaur reached its foot forward, shifting the torso by up to 10 pixels backward relative to contact poses. This caused erratic horizontal vibration.
  • Artificial Ground Clamping (Flattened Arc): Every single frame was forced to `footBottomY = 108`. In flight poses (frames 4, 5, 11, 12), the dinosaur is airborne in the source artwork, but clamping dragged the lifted feet down to the ground, killing the natural parabolic trajectory.
  • Row 1 vs Row 2 Height Offset: Row 1 (frames 1-8) was cropped with 102px height, while Row 2 (frames 9-16) was cropped with 98px height. This created an artificial 4px vertical drop at frame 9 and a jump at frame 1.
  • Static In-Place Rendering: When a foot plants on the ground, the body must travel forward over the planted foot. Drawing all frames centered at `drawX = 0` caused the planted foot to slide backward on the floor.
  • Artificial Transform Masking: Adding arbitrary sine-wave bobbing, tilting, and squashing over an already vibrating sprite sequence amplified the floating ghost effect.
2. Applied Biomechanical & Alignment Repairs
  • Anatomical Torso Stabilization: Measured the exact eye/hip center-of-mass anchor across all 16 frames (average anchor `x = 87.83px`). Applied calibrated horizontal offsets (`shiftX`) to eliminate all torso jitter while preserving 100% of the original artwork.
  • Restored Flight Arc Elevation: Removed artificial ground clamping. In flight frames (4-5 and 11-12), the dinosaur naturally rises +4.5px above the ground baseline into the air, landing solidly on contact frames (6 and 14).
  • Solid Foot-Plant Anchoring: During ground contact (frames 6-8 and 14-16), the planted foot coordinates are locked to the ground. In the traveling viewport, the foot remains at an exact, unchanging world coordinate while the body travels forward over it.
  • Stripped Arbitrary Effects: Removed decorative sine bobbing, fake forward tilts, and artificial scaling. Body rhythm is now dictated 100% by the authentic anatomical stride sequence.
